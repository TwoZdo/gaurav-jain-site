Money, Marketed

A Primer on the Marketing of Financial Products

Financial products are different. They are often complex, intangible, and deeply connected to risk, trust, and long-term financial decisions.

Money, Marketed explores how financial products are developed, positioned, communicated, and adopted, and why understanding their markets is essential to their success.
