Beyond the Product: Three Questions That Matter

Dr. Jain’s lens has three parts: the product, the customer, and the market.

The product

Start with what the product actually does.

  • What problem does it solve?
  • How does it create value?
  • What makes it different?
The customer

See it the way the customer sees it.

  • Who needs it?
  • How do customers perceive its benefits, risks, and complexity?
The market

Then read the market it enters.

  • Where is the opportunity?
  • What drives adoption, competition, and sustainable growth?
