# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS (no framework) for the first drafts. The site will later be rebuilt in a no-code builder, so keep it simple and easy to port.

## Users

Decision-makers who book speakers and training: L&D and HR leads, executive-education directors, and event and association organizers at banks, credit unions, asset managers, insurers, fintechs, and PE portfolio companies. They visit to judge credibility and fit, then get in touch.

## Product Purpose

The speaker and advisor site of Dr. Gaurav Jain. It should turn a visitor into an inquiry for a talk, workshop, or executive-education program, and show the book *Money, Marketed* as the intellectual anchor.

## Positioning

A tenured professor (Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute) and author who connects financial products with marketing and behavior. His three-part lens is product, customer, and market. Most finance speakers cover markets; he covers why financial products get adopted or fail.

## Brand Commitments

- Name style: "Dr. Gaurav Jain". Book title: *Money, Marketed: A Primer on the Marketing of Financial Products*.
- Book quotes go in dedicated pull-quote breaks. The author will supply the final quotes.
- Visual direction chosen by the user on 2026-11-19: Financial Broadsheet (salmon newsprint, column rules, headline serif), replacing the earlier lecture-hall editorial look.
- Restyled by the user later on 2026-11-19 (v18): navy text on off-white, a hint of yellow, slow rippling ring patterns, General Sans only (no serif, no mono). Broadsheet layout and structure kept.

## Product Principles

- Credibility through evidence (photos, the book, institutions), never inflated claims.
- One clear action: invite Dr. Jain.
- Use plain language that is respectful of a professional audience.
